# Product Requirements Document: My Family Tree App

## App Overview
**Name:** My Family
**Goal:** A lightweight, easy-to-use family tree builder that works in browsers as a webapp. No login required.

## Key Features
1. **No-Login Interface:** Immediate access to the family tree builder.
2. **Easy Tree Creation:** Visual drag-and-drop or simple form-based node addition.
3. **Local Database:** Data is stored locally in the browser (IndexedDB/LocalStorage).
4. **Export/Import:** 
   - Export to JSON/CSV for mobile storage.
   - Sync/Backup to Google Drive.
   - Import feature to restore or update the tree.
5. **Bottom Navigation:** Quick access to Tree, Search, Media/Gallery, and Settings.
6. **Admin Panel:** A separate interface for managing app content, global settings, or viewing anonymized stats.

## Design Principles
- **Clean & Attractive:** Minimalist but colorful.
- **Fast:** Optimized for mobile browser performance.
- **Universal Design:** Usable by all age groups.

## Proposed Screens
1. **Home/Splash:** Welcome screen with options to "Start New Tree" or "Import".
2. **Main Tree View:** The interactive family tree canvas.
3. **Member Detail/Add:** A clean form to add/edit family member details (Photo, Name, Relation, Bio).
4. **Settings & Data:** Export/Import controls, Google Drive sync, and app preferences.
5. **Admin Dashboard:** Separate panel for content management.
